## My interests

There's only one problem that interests me, and that is **problem solving** itself. 

Specificially, I want to approach problem solving (under uncertain information) from a technical perspective. This involves: 
- ML architecture and interpretability: This works! Great! Why? 
- Metacognition: I think I think. So, algorithms think on algorithms; how can agents self improve? 
- Knowledge bases: A cached solution is a O(1) lookup. How can we get a knowledge base to store and reason effectively? 

I am interested in the following fields: 
- Computer Science (ML, theory): Developing algorithms to encapture and prove the methods of reasoning and deduction.
- Math (linalg, stats): Using mathematics as a basis for philosophical proofs on human behavior, on bot the micro (individual) and macro (society) levels.
- Business (theory): a very nice source of Games

Some other adjacent fields that provoke my interest:
- Applets and scripting: end-to-end pipelines for user experience; workflow optimizations
- Computer Engineering: designing the compute that makes training and inference possible

If you share these interests, feel free to send me an email!
